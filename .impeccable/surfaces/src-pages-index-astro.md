# Surface brief: GENCI landing page (src/pages/index.astro)

## Scope
Single-page landing page. Mode: Persuade. Mobile first (375, 768, 1440).

## Audience, job, action
Busy owner-operator of a Bay Area home service business (cleaning, remodeling, carpet; also landscaping, roofing, pressure washing, HVAC, painting), usually on a phone, often cold traffic. One action: request a free sample edit ("Get my free sample edit") via a 4-field Formspree form; Call / Text as secondary paths in the final section, footer and sticky mobile bar.

## Proof and content
Real client videos exist and will be supplied; until then placeholder videos. Proof strip, pricing, turnaround, revisions, sample scope and response time are visible [NEED: ...] markers. Never invent stats, names, logos, reviews or guarantees.

## Constraints
Strict black/white/grays, color only inside video frames. TeX Gyre Adventor for headlines, nav, buttons; Outfit body; Syne for single highlighted words, numbers, labels. Header holds logo + CTA only. No address anywhere; areaServed only.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is a route under overhead signs. Each section is one junction with one sign: one message, one arrow, one next step, and the route ends at the gate, the free sample edit. It refuses the category default of a centered headline over a row of phone mockups, a logo strip and icon cards.

OWN-WORLD: White concourse ground. Sign panels in ink black #0a0a0a with white sentence-case Avant Garde, hung as full-bleed bands framed by a 1px graphite rule (#3a3a3a). White pictogram squares carry black arrows locked to the panel edge they point past. Step and gate numbers are monumental Syne numerals in black squares. Grays come in four fixed steps (#f2f2f2, #d4d4d4, #737373, #3a3a3a) and nothing between. Color lives only inside video frames, which hang like concourse monitors. Buttons are small sign panels ending in an arrow square. Translation: the catalog's airport yellow becomes ink black, as the brief pins the palette.

STORY: The owner reads one sign at a time: what this is (videos edited from your job-site phone clips) and who it is for (Bay Area cleaning, remodeling, carpet), then sees their own niche on the departures board, the raw-vs-edited proof, the three-step route, what is included, the starting price, answers to their doubts, and reaches the gate: request a free sample edit by form, call or text.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Mobile 390: black overhead header band (logo inverted, small white sign button). Full-bleed black sign panel: white arrow pictogram square, Syne kicker, headline ~34px white, one-line niche label. Under it, three route rows on white (black numeral square 1/2/3, message, edge arrow). Then the gate band: black, "Get my free sample edit" with white arrow square, all above the fold. The hero reel (9:16 monitor) starts just below. Desktop 1440: header band; the sign panel spans the 12-col grid with headline cols 1-8 at ~84px and subline; the reel hangs as a 9:16 monitor in cols 10-12 inside the panel; below the panel the three route signs (cols 1-8) sit beside the gate band (cols 9-12).

FORM: Fused catalog challenger "international airport sign system" (dealt challenger 4, verdict competitive, chosen by the user over the assigned direction), fused with the product's own facts. Seed key 61f1bc43. Signature interaction: the niche selector is a departures board whose destination band split-flaps to the chosen niche and swaps the vertical-video carousel and its copy line. Motion grammar: split-flap band updates and signs entering from their arrow's direction, transform and opacity only, static under reduced motion; the How-it-works corridor pins and walks the three numbered signs.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Picking "Carpet cleaning" on the departures board: the band flaps to the new destination and the monitors switch to carpet jobs.
